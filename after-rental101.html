Important: Where multiple departments are involved and a GDPR breach has taken place, it is YOUR responsibility to manage it. Do not assume another department will act. Make Executive Relations aware, including any duplicate cases.

Subject Access Request (SAR)

Reason Codes: After Rental > Data Protection > Access/Copy of my Data

Who manages: The country where the customer last hired is responsible. Allocate to that country's Salesforce queue, or email the country if non-corporate.

UK only: Send to executive.relations@europcar.com — NOT to Nick Eland / Lucy Brown. ALL SAR requests must be checked by a Team Leader before sending to Executive Relations.

Phone:

  1. Complete full Data Protection check.
  2. Create a case with correct reason codes.
  3. Ascertain which country should manage (last rental country).
  4. Email customer to acknowledge and advise SLA.
  5. Send email from case to executive.relations@europcar.com (reference Case number and date of request).
  6. Cancel the case — do NOT close it.

Email:

  1. Forward from CRM tool to executive.relations@europcar.com.
  2. Email customer to acknowledge; include privacy policy link.
  3. Cancel the case — do NOT close it.

Right of Erasure (ROE)

Reason Codes: After Rental > Data Protection > Right of Erasure
Cannot delete if: Customer has outstanding balances, is watchlisted, or has an ongoing case. If a complaint also exists, CS must continue to manage and solve it — only the ROE request transfers to the country.

UK only: Email case number and details to executive.relations@europcar.com. Acknowledge customer. Cancel the case.

Other countries: Allocate to relevant country queue (email if non-corporate). Add full case comments including what is to be deleted, why, and who requested it. Escalation flag: Escalation to Country.

Reasons an ID cannot be deleted:

  • Outstanding balances — customer must pay first.
  • Outstanding investigations (CS, Insurance, PCN) — must be resolved first.
  • Watchlisted — cannot remove until watchlist is resolved.

Erase from CRM Tool Only

If customer has a Driver Account on CRM tool but no Driver ID: send all deletion requests to executive.relations@europcar.com. They manage deletion with the requester.

Credit Card Details

We hold card details for 13 months after last transaction (per Privacy Policy, section 5).

  • 13 months passed: Raise IT ticket in Service Now (SNow) for erasure. Include customer details and email showing the request. Cancel case while waiting. Monitor progress.
  • Not yet 13 months: Advise customer removal not possible. Use existing CRM template.

Right of a Deceased Person

Reason Codes: After Rental > Data Protection > Right of a deceased person

All requests must come in writing. A copy of the death certificate is required.

Calls:

Advise caller to put request in writing. Create a case on the call and send an email for the caller to respond to. Cancel case until response received.

Once death certificate received:

Confirm details are correct. Investigate as normal. Closing — Outcome: dependent on reason; Root Cause: dependent on responsible party.

County Court Judgment (CCJ)

Any correspondence referencing: HM Courts and Tribunals Service, HMCTS, Order, Judgement, Moneyclaim, words "Claimant / Defendant / Respondent", or bearing a County Court seal.

Action: Forward immediately to executive.relations@europcar.com with lucy.brown@europcar.com in copy. Cancel the case.

Restrict or Object to Use of My Data

Reason Codes: After Rental > Data Protection > Restrict or Object Use of My Data
  • Fines: Pass to UK Fines queue — they have their own internal policies.
  • Outstanding Balances: T&C Section 21.2 — customer accepted details can be passed to a Debt Collection Agency for outstanding amounts.

Marketing Consent / Unsubscribe

Reason Codes: Before Rental > Data Protection > Unsubscribe/Consent Withdraw

Can be done over phone or email. Advise customer they can self-manage via account login. Update both CRM tool and Greenway.

Days Charged

Reason Codes: After Rental > Invoice Dispute > Extra Services > Non-Compliance with Rental Agreement
Send RFI when:
  • Customer claims early check-out was permitted at no charge.
  • Customer claims station agreed not to charge additional day.
  • Customer is adamant they returned on time.
Maintain when:
  • Sufficient documented evidence available.
  • Customer accepts late return, only disputes the amount.
  • Customer accepts late return, only disputes grace period.
Refund when: Sufficient evidence vehicle returned on time. RFI confirms additional day charged in error. No RFI response and/or insufficient documentation.

Systems to check:

  • Greenway F7 notes on invoice, Vehicle Movement Screen
  • Connexus — may show actual return time, counteracting Greenway check-in time

Additional Days — Amount Dispute

We cannot charge more than the amount shown on the rental agreement unless the customer has signed a new RA. T&C: customer is liable for all charges until vehicle returned. Check RA and SOR to see if customer signed for additional day at start or accepted fee on return.

Prepaid Early Return — Daily Rate Increased

Happens when package rental (7/14/28 days) is returned early — system reverts to a different daily rate. We cannot charge a higher daily rate than the RA. Revert invoice to original prepaid dates on Greenway. If not possible, calculate manually and refund as allowance or via form.

Prepaid Early Return — Unused Days Refund

T&C: We do not refund for unused days. Exception only: early return due to mechanical/breakdown (not keys lost/damaged/locked in). Charge back to return station as it should not have amended the charge date/time.

Extension Not Authorised

Reason Codes: During Rental Experience > Rental Extension > Extension Not Authorised

For TO bookings: station charges daily rate on date of extension. Voucher coverage: if TO emails us accepting additional days, amend voucher in DEBCRED. If customer claims TO should cover: refer customer back to TO. If TO contacts us, we can amend accordingly.
